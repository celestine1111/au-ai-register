Open data on Australian migration, government AI, and which governments actually publish their numbers. Every row carries the URL it came from and the date it was retrieved. Nothing here asks you to take our word for it.

Stock, not flow. This counts residents at a point in time, so change nets out departures and deaths and is not the same as arrivals. The 2016–2021 interval also contains roughly eighteen months of closed borders, which suppresses growth against a normal period.

3. Origin countries do publish emigration data. Australia is simply not in it.

The common claim that migrant-origin countries hide their numbers is false. South Asian labour-sending states run some of the most detailed emigration registers anywhere, several updated monthly. Australia is near-absent from every one of them, and the reason is not concealment.

These are labour clearance systems, and that is the whole explanation. Each one clears a worker to depart under a vetted foreign employment contract, and each exists because the destination is a jurisdiction where the sending state judges its nationals to need protection. India's Emigration Check Required list names 18 countries designated on the basis that they "do not have strict laws regulating the entry and employment of foreign nationals". Australia is not among them, so an Indian moving to Australia generates no record at all.

So these figures are not corridor sizes, and must never be quoted as if they were. Bangladesh recording 70 does not contradict Australia recording far more Bangladesh-born arrivals. Students, skilled independent migrants and family arrivals are invisible to a labour bureau by construction, and those routes carry almost everyone who reaches Australia.

4. Australia's own figures, checked against someone who can contradict them

Every headline migration number in Australian debate is produced by Australia. That is a fair objection, and migration cannot be audited the way trade is, because trade has two records of one shipment and migration has one: almost no state records the departure of its own nationals. The available check is a third party estimating every country on one method, with no stake in the Australian argument. That is the UN Population Division, via the World Bank.

Every comparison, with the agreement band fixed at plus or minus 5 per cent before the test was run, in data/corroboration.csv.

Australia's count of who lives here is independently confirmed. Its annual flow figure is not, and the decisive case is the pandemic. For the year to June 2021 the ABS records net migration of -84,930, Australia losing people with the border closed. The UN series records +116,768 for 2021.

That is not a scandal on either side. ABS measures border crossings directly under the 12/16 month rule. The UN derives net migration residually from a population balance and smooths it across multi-year periods, which is right for comparing 200 countries and structurally incapable of seeing a border shut in one year.

The rule that follows: for an annual Australian migration figure, cite ABS. For cross-country comparison or resident migrant population, the UN series is legitimate corroboration and Australia passes it. Quoting the UN figure against ABS is a misuse of it.

5. Australia has already run negative net migration

In the year to June 2021 Australia recorded net overseas migration of -84,930. A reduced or negative intake is not a hypothetical for this country; it is measured, recent, and in the ABS series.

This was caused by pandemic border closure, not policy design, and included departures of temporary visa holders that would not recur under a planned reduction. It is precedent for the measurement, not a model of the outcome. From ABS_NOM_VISA_FY, in data/corroboration.csv.

6. AI-attributed job losses are harder to verify than the headlines suggest

Australian cases in the current seed that are employer-stated, undisputed, and onshore: zero. Commonwealth Bank withdrew a 45-role attribution as an "error"; 176 technology roles are contested by the Finance Sector Union as offshoring; the chat support cuts were at a contractor in Johannesburg. Telstra cut more than 1,000 roles and stated it was "not proposing that any roles be replaced by AI".

In data/ai-attributed-job-changes.csv, with attribution tier, dispute notes and employer denials recorded separately. Four rows is a seed, not an exhaustive search: this is "no clean case found yet", not "no clean case exists".

Provenance

Three columns appear in every dataset and are enforced by continuous integration:

ColumnMeaning
source_urlThe exact URL the row came from. For API-derived rows this is the precise query, so it can be re-run to reproduce the figure.
source_retrieved_atISO 8601 retrieval timestamp.
source_tierprimary for a public record, claim for a company announcement that is public but promotional.

The build fails on a blank provenance column, an invalid tier, or a source_url that is a bare homepage. A front page changes daily and cannot evidence a historical figure. That gate has already rejected two of our own defective datasets before they reached the default branch.

Verify anything here

curl -H "Accept: application/vnd.sdmx.data+csv;labels=both" \
  "https://data.api.abs.gov.au/rest/data/ABS,ABS_NOM_VISA_FY,1.0.0/all?startPeriod=2015&format=csv"

Every ingest is re-runnable and idempotent. If a figure cannot be reproduced from its source_url, that is a defect: open an issue.
